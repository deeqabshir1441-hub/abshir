Contact TV96 Live

Questions, feedback, and content concerns

Email us at support@tv96live.org.

General Enquiries

For questions or feedback about TV96 Live, email support@tv96live.org. Please provide enough detail for us to understand your enquiry.

Corrections and Editorial Feedback

If you believe a news article, fixture, score, standing, or other published item contains a factual error, contact support@tv96live.org. Include the page URL, the information you believe is incorrect, and any relevant source that can help us review the issue.

Copyright and Rights Concerns

If you believe content published on TV96 Live affects rights you own or control, please contact us with sufficient information so the matter can be reviewed.

Email support@tv96live.org and identify the material, its page URL, the rights involved, and your relationship to those rights. Do not send confidential credentials or passwords.

Technical Issues

To report a broken page, display problem, or other technical issue, email support@tv96live.org. Include the affected URL, what happened, and the browser or device you were using where relevant.

TV96 Live does not currently provide a web contact form. The email links above open your device's configured email application.

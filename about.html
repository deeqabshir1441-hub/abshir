Who We Are

TV96 Live is a football information website for supporters who want to follow competitions, clubs, matches, and developments in the game. The site brings together match information and football-related editorial content in one place.

We aim to present information in a clear and accessible format. TV96 Live is not described as an official league, club, governing-body, or broadcasting partner.

What We Cover

Our football coverage may include:

  • Fixtures, match schedules, and score updates.
  • League tables and standings.
  • Football news and transfer developments.
  • Match previews and competition information.
  • Football-related articles, context, and commentary.

Availability and update times can vary by competition and by the source of the underlying information. Match schedules, scores, and standings can also change after publication.

Our Editorial Approach

Match information may be obtained from reputable sports data providers and publicly available football sources. Where appropriate, editorial articles may also refer to statements from clubs, competitions, players, or other public sources.

Editorial content should be reviewed before publication for clarity, relevance, and factual consistency. News reporting and commentary should be distinguishable where the difference is relevant, and uncertain or developing information should not be presented as confirmed fact.

Football information can develop quickly. Readers should check relevant clubs, competitions, or governing bodies when they need official confirmation.

Accuracy and Corrections

TV96 Live aims to keep published information accurate and current, but errors, delays, or incomplete information may occasionally occur. Scores, schedules, standings, and developing news can be revised as new information becomes available.

Readers can report a possible factual error by emailing support@tv96live.org. Please include the page address and a clear description of the issue. When a correction can be verified, the relevant information may be updated.

Our Values

  • Clarity: Present football information in an understandable format.
  • Accuracy: Check factual information and correct verified errors.
  • Transparency: Distinguish confirmed information from commentary or developing reports.
  • Respect: Cover clubs, players, competitions, and readers responsibly.
  • Usefulness: Focus on information that helps readers follow football.

Our Vision

Our goal is to develop TV96 Live into a useful and easy-to-navigate football resource. We intend to improve the depth, presentation, and consistency of our match information and editorial coverage over time.

Contact

For general questions, feedback, or correction requests, contact TV96 Live at support@tv96live.org.

When contacting us about a specific page, please include its URL so the request can be reviewed more efficiently.
